#### How semiconductor is made from inside, why is it semi?
It's made out of SEMIconduction materials. These materials group are neither good conductors like Cooper, nor absolute isolators like Glass.
<br/>
The most common of these is Silicon.
<br/>
But to properly tranfer the electric current it needs a mix with a little amount of materials that will in combination give:
- 1 electron(-) extra. *N-type transistor is formed.*
- 1 electron lacking = free(+). *P-type transistor is formed.*

Combination of N-type and P-type regions create the working transistor.

> WARNING!
> There's a misconseption that N-type transistors are negitively charged and P-type transistors positively charged. In reality they are neutral, because the amount of protons and electrons in them are equal and has regions with (-) and (+) current. [*3](#sources)
